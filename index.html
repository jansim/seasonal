      The paintings

      The illustrations are watercolours from the USDA Pomological Watercolor Collection: some 7,500 paintings of fruit and nut varieties, commissioned by the U.S. Department of Agriculture between 1886 and 1942 to document new and introduced cultivars. They are in the public domain. Here they have been cut from their paper with an image segmentation model (BiRefNet). The collection holds no vegetables apart from a single aubergine, and no rhubarb or apricot.

      U.S. Department of Agriculture Pomological Watercolor Collection. Rare and Special Collections, National Agricultural Library, Beltsville, MD 20705.
